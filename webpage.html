About Our Company

WELSPUN CORP is a global leader in industrial manufacturing and infrastructure solutions. Driven by a culture of continuous evolution, we combine engineering excellence, advanced digital technology, and deeply sustainable practices to build infrastructure that shapes industries across continents. From heavy industrial manufacturing to next-generation material science, we empower economies and support communities worldwide.

Our Mission

To provide world-class, tech-driven solutions that maximize stakeholder value, pioneer industrial innovation, and champion sustainable regional growth across all our operational hubs.

Our Vision

To remain a globally trusted industry benchmark recognized for manufacturing breakthroughs, ethical environmental responsibility, and a relentless commitment to engineering precision.

Our Services

Manufacturing

Operating state-of-the-art automated manufacturing lines that comply with top-tier global testing certifications to deliver high-pressure industrial line pipes and automated structures flawlessly.

Infrastructure & Tech

Deploying smart industrial automation, specialized ductile iron water systems, and advanced corporate data security workflows to build resilient layouts for smart city networks.

Sustainability
